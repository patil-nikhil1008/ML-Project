1. Spatial Centrality (25% Weight) Compositional Framing

Measures normalized Euclidean distance from the bounding box centroid to the optical image center. Main subjects are framed centrally; photobombers appear near edges and corners.

Score = 1.0 - (Dist(center, img_center) / max_diagonal)

2. Relative Size (25% Weight) Perspective Scale

Computes bounding box area as a ratio against the largest detected human. Foreground subjects dominate the visual field; background lurkers occupy tiny fractions.

Score = Area(person_i) / max(Area(all_persons))

3. Focus & Sharpness (20% Weight) Laplacian Variance

Calculates edge gradient variance using the discrete Laplacian operator on grayscale crops. Autofocus sharpens subjects; intruders outside depth-of-field are blurred.

Score = Var(Laplacian(gray_crop)) / max_sharpness

4. Monocular Depth Disparity (15% Weight) MiDaS Deep Disparity

Estimates dense relative depth. Sampling the median disparity in the central 60% of the bounding box isolates the primary foreground plane from background planes.

Score = Rescale(Median(DisparityMap[bbox_interior]))

5. Pose Landmark Visibility & Torso Orientation (15% Weight) MediaPipe Pose

Extracts 33 human anatomical joints. Measures visibility of shoulders, hips, and facial landmarks. Posing subjects face forward; photobombers frequently turn away, duck, or have occluded joints.

Score = 0.5 * avg_visibility + (0.2 if frontal_shoulders else 0.0) + 0.3
